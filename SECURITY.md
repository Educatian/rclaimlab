# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately to the maintainer email in `DESCRIPTION`. Do not include learner records, credentials, or identifiable research data in an issue.

Supported versions are the current CRAN release and the active release candidate. The team will acknowledge a report within five business days, assess impact, prepare a fix and disclosure timeline, and rotate affected credentials. Browser receipts are local by default, and R-ClaimLab does not collect telemetry.
