# R-LearnXR Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in R-LearnXR to be safe, respectful, and useful for people with different levels of R, teaching, language, and accessibility experience.

## Expected behavior

- Give feedback about work, not personal traits.
- Explain assumptions and avoid treating prior technical knowledge as a requirement for belonging.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing learner data or quotations.
- Provide accessible materials and welcome reports about barriers.
- Credit contributors and data sources accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate accessibility exclusion, and retaliation are not accepted in project spaces.

## Reporting

Until a public repository and named community team are established, report conduct concerns privately to the project lead listed in the grant proposal. The public release must replace this sentence with a monitored contact and documented response process.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
