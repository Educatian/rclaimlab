# Assessment Rubric — Reusable R-LearnXR Exit Ticket

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. A formative “ready” response usually scores at least 7/10 and has no zero in evidence use.

| Dimension | 0 — not yet | 1 — developing | 2 — demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statistical concept | Names PCA or a coordinate without explaining it | Gives a partly accurate explanation of a component or score | Explains a score as a position along a multivariate direction |
| R/data reasoning | Cannot identify the input or transformation | Identifies one relevant step such as scaling or filtering | Explains why the variables, scaling, or sample affect interpretation |
| Evidence use | Makes an unsupported visual claim | Cites a point or axis but not both clearly | Names a point, component, direction, and approximate coordinate |
| Uncertainty/limitation | Overclaims a cause or universal rule | Mentions one limitation without connecting it to the claim | Explains what the evidence supports and what remains unknown |
| Transfer/reproducibility | Does not compare a new point or cannot locate the source | Compares a new point or identifies one receipt field | Transfers the reasoning and identifies code, seed/runtime, and artifact evidence |

## Instructor use

Use the rubric for feedback, not grading a learner's identity or ability. Store only aggregated scores for a pilot unless learners separately consent to sharing their text. A score is not an estimate of learning effectiveness without a study design.
