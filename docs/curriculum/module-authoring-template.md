## Activity sequence

1. **Orient:** name the decision and define one row, one variable, and the evidence boundary.
2. **Predict:** ask for a falsifiable expectation before R runs.
3. **Run R:** keep the starter pipeline short and expose the transformation.
4. **Explore:** use the scene, semantic table, or keyboard path to inspect evidence.
5. **Explain:** require a claim, coordinate/statistic, and interpretation.
6. **Transfer:** compare a new observation, group, sample, or parameter choice.
7. **Reproduce:** export source and receipt; inspect seed, environment, and artifact hash.

## Assessment

- Conceptual claim
- R/data reasoning
- Evidence use
- Limitation or uncertainty
- Transfer/reproducibility

Do not convert a formative score into a claim of learning effectiveness without a human pilot and an appropriate study design.
