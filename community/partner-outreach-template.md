# Pilot Partner Outreach Template

Subject: Pilot an open R + Quarto 3D learning workflow

R-ClaimLab is an open-source R package and Quarto lesson framework for reproducible browser-based 3D data exploration. We are seeking a small number of R educators and learner communities to test one eight-minute lesson and the lesson-authoring workflow.

The pilot asks learners to predict, inspect, explain, and transfer a data interpretation. Instructors test scaffolding, rendering, and reproducibility checks. Participation does not require a headset. Keyboard and data-table alternatives are included.

We will publish only aggregated findings and issue-level recommendations. Would your group be willing to provide five learner sessions, one instructor review, or a short statement describing a likely reuse case?
