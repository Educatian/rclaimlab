# Formative learner and instructor protocol

## Learner session, 45 minutes

1. Consent and background, 5 minutes.
2. Think-aloud Orient and Predict, 5 minutes.
3. Run R and recover from one planned error, 10 minutes.
4. Explore with assigned table/keyboard or visual path, 8 minutes.
5. Explain, receive criterion feedback, and Repair, 8 minutes.
6. Transfer and Reproduce, 6 minutes.
7. Short interview, 3 minutes.

Collect task completion, critical incidents, rubric scores, and self-reported effort. Do not collect identifiable data in browser receipts.

## Instructor session, 60 minutes

Ask the instructor to install from a clean environment, adapt one dataset, modify one rubric criterion, rebuild the lesson, inspect accessibility fallbacks, and interpret the check report. Record API friction, missing documentation, and classroom deployment concerns.

## Severity

- P0: data loss, privacy leak, inaccessible required pathway, invalid statistical evidence.
- P1: learner or instructor cannot complete a core task without facilitator intervention.
- P2: confusing, slow, or inconsistent behavior with a documented workaround.
- P3: polish or preference.
