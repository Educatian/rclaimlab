## What prevents an honest 5/5 across all criteria

Two criteria depend on external evidence rather than additional code:

1. Yeonji Jung must confirm public attribution, January-June 2027 availability, Milestone 4 ownership, and any allowed compensation arrangement in writing.
2. R educators or maintainers should review the need, API, scope, and reuse plan.
3. Learners and instructors should complete the documented feasibility protocol and leave attributable evidence where permission is granted.
4. A real screen-reader user or accessibility reviewer should complete the non-visual path; automated checks cannot establish that evidence.

These cannot be replaced by synthetic personas, internal testing, or screenshots. The repository includes the outreach template, pilot protocol, and validation-status file needed to collect them before submission. A co-lead is not a prerequisite, but external validation remains important evidence for community impact.
